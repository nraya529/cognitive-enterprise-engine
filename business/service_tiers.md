# Service Tiers & Pricing

The two-tier structure exists for one reason: it makes the lower tier feel like the safe choice, which is exactly what most first-time AI buyers want. Almost everyone picks Tier A first. About 30% upgrade to Tier B within 6 months once they see the system work.

## Tier A — The Autonomous Pivot

**$5,000 setup · $450/month**

For: one painful workflow, eliminated cleanly.

**Includes:**
- Discovery, scope, and architecture document
- Integration with **one** accounting platform (QuickBooks Online, Xero, FreshBooks, Wave)
- **One** ingestion channel (forwarded email address, upload portal, or webhook)
- Document types: **2** (typically invoices + receipts)
- Verification & self-healing pipeline
- Vendor matching against client's existing vendor list (one-time import)
- Auto-classification against client's existing chart of accounts
- Slack OR email escalation channel for exceptions
- Two-week deployment timeline
- 30-day satisfaction guarantee — full refund of setup if not operational

**Monthly retainer covers:**
- Hosting (cloud server, API costs absorbed)
- Logs, monitoring, and uptime
- Up to 2 hours of small tuning/adjustment requests per month
- Email support, 1 business day response time

**Excludes:**
- Net-new automation workflows (additional scope = additional setup fee)
- Direct phone support outside scheduled reviews
- Custom dashboards or reporting

## Tier B — Full Cognitive Transformation

**$9,500 setup · $750/month**

For: clients ready to eliminate back-office friction across the board.

**Includes everything in Tier A, plus:**
- Multi-channel ingestion (email + upload + SMS + vendor portals)
- All document types: invoices, receipts, purchase orders, W-9s, HR onboarding, expense reports
- Vendor onboarding workflow including automated W-9 chase
- Cash flow forecasting dashboard (12-week rolling)
- Anomaly detection with risk scoring
- Quarterly performance review and prompt optimization session
- Priority queue for new automation requests (24-hour scoping turnaround)
- Direct line: same-day response to urgent questions
- 60-day satisfaction guarantee

**Monthly retainer covers:**
- Everything in Tier A's retainer
- Up to 6 hours of new automation work per month
- Quarterly in-person or video review with deliverables
- First-line access to new model capabilities as they ship

## Pricing Philosophy

The temptation when starting out is to undercharge. Resist it.

- **Below $2,500 you cannot afford the discovery time.** A real discovery call + custom proposal + setup easily eats 12-15 hours of skilled work. At $2,500 you're at $165/hr before any maintenance. At $1,500 you're at $100/hr and you'll burn out.
- **Below $250/month retainer the client treats you as disposable.** They will churn the moment something better appears. Higher retainers create stickier clients, not the reverse.
- **One $5,000 client beats four $1,500 clients.** The $5K client expects you to think; the $1,500 client expects you to fix tickets. Different business model, different lifestyle.

Stay disciplined. Walk away from clients who push you below floor pricing.

## Math: getting to $10K/month

Two paths:

**Path 1 — New deals**
- 2 new Tier A clients per month at $5,000 setup = $10,000 setup revenue.
- Backlog of recurring retainers accrues underneath. By month 6: 12 clients × $450 = $5,400/month recurring, on top of new setups.

**Path 2 — Retainer focus**
- After 18 months at 2 new clients per month with ~10% churn, you'll stabilize around 18-20 active retainers.
- 18 × $450 = $8,100/month recurring. Add 1 new setup at $5K to clear $13K with predictable cashflow.

Path 2 is the better long-term play. Path 1 is what gets you out of the gate.
